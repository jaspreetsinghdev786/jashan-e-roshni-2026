Common questions

Who can take part?

The Day 1 finals (5 November) are open to students from all departments of MRSPTU. Mehndi (28 October) and Day 2 events (6 November) are for Computational Sciences students.

How do I register?

Use the Register button on any page. It opens the official Google Form. Entries for the Day 1 inter-department competitions (5 November) close on 2 November 2026.

What should I bring?

Your own materials and tools: paints, fabric, diyas, scrap material and so on. Each event's rules list what you need. Rules are on the Events page.

Can I sell my work at the exhibition?

Yes. Selling is optional. You make your own price tag with your name, roll number, event name and price, and handle the sale yourself. See the Exhibition page.

When are the results?

Friday 6 November, 3:15 PM to 4:00 PM, with trophies, certificates and awards for 1st and 2nd place.
