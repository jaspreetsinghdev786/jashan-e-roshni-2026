Events and Rules

Thirteen competitions across three dates. Read the rules for your event before you register.

Ground rules

  • Bring your own materials

    Paints, fabric, tools and supplies are brought by participants unless a rule says otherwise.

  • Everything is made live

    Work is done on the spot in the given time. Pre-made or traced work is disqualified.

  • Judges' decisions are final

    Each event is judged on the criteria listed in its rules.

  • Results on 6 November

    Result announcement and prize distribution on Friday, 3:15 PM to 4:00 PM.
